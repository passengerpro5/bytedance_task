# Campaign Boundaries

Use this skill for every Script Agent generation.

## Scope

- Use only approved source breakdowns, reference storyboards, Moras assets, selected personas, and request metadata.
- Do not invent external comment, review, Reddit, Quora, customer, follower, GMV, income, watch-time, conversion, or performance evidence.
- If the requested type is `Comment Reply`, do not pretend a real comment exists. Reframe it as an approved objection-response format unless the input explicitly provides a source comment.
- Treat Moras reference examples as social distribution videos, not paid-software ad templates. Output should feel creator-native and persona-led.

## Moras Product Boundary

Allowed Moras value:
- Discover / product library / product selection.
- One-click `Generate`, `Create video`, or `Custom Create` product-to-video workflow.
- Completed product video with a visible commerce path.
- One-click publish or TikTok posting entry inside the real client.
- Drafts / Posts management, pre-check, text edits, split/delete salvage, full-video regeneration.
- Data / GMV / items-sold / commission feedback when framed as observed Moras-published-content evidence.
- Time saved, fewer blank-page scripts, more product-to-video output, and creator earning opportunity without fixed results.

Product truth to protect:
- Moras is a creator monetization product and commercial AI employee, not a pre-sample testing utility.
- The user-facing loop is Discover -> Create video -> video management -> Data feedback.
- The growth script must sell the ability to turn selected products into videos that can be posted and monetized, while avoiding fixed-income guarantees.

MUST NOT:
- Frame Moras as a pre-shoot, pre-sample, or before-recording planning aid.
- Frame Moras as a "test before buying samples" tool, sample-buying helper, sample-cost reducer, or sample decision aid.
- Use `buy samples`, `sample box`, `sample excuse`, `before I buy samples`, `before I spend on samples`, `No Sample Needed`, `one more test`, or `test smarter` as publishable story language.
- Let "testing" become the main value proposition in spoken copy. Internal strategy can say reduced trial-and-error; publishable copy should say post more product videos, promote products, and create more chances to earn.
- Say or imply the creator still needs to manually film the Moras output afterward.
- Say `rough cut`, `editable rough cut`, `instant draft`, `directly ready to edit`, or `just dump product info`.
- Say Moras accepts arbitrary pasted product data unless a future approved capability says so.
- Promise fixed income, sales, passive income, automatic money, settlement timing, platform bypass, video quality, realism, polish, or guaranteed performance.
- Put forbidden-claim labels in publishable fields. Risk notes may say safer meta-language such as `unsupported outcome promise`, `sales promise`, or `no fixed result`.

## Campaign Standard

Target audience:
- 5k+ TikTok Shop affiliate creators, or TikTok Shop creators who have not made real money yet.

Core promise:
- Creators no longer manually dig for products, write scripts from zero, guess what works, or rebuild videos from scratch.
- Moras shows promising products, lets them choose a product, hit Generate, review the completed video, and move toward posting with the commerce path visible.
- This is the product capability boundary, not the required spoken wording. In publishable voiceover and caption highlights, say it like a creator: choose a product, hit Generate, Moras makes the video, check it, then post it.

Recommended timeline:
- 0-5s: urgent creator-facing Hook.
- 5-16s: pain or proof, such as creator result clips, handle screenshots, GMV screenshots, or the cost of manual guessing.
- 16-32s: digital human or live creator plus full-screen Moras screen recording.
- 32-40s: result reinforcement with approved proof screenshots or time proof.
- 40-43s: direct click-button CTA.

Screen-recording beats:
- Open Moras.
- Pause one beat.
- Choose a product.
- Scroll product library.
- Select a product.
- Tap Generate.
- Wait one beat.
- Show the completed product video and posting entry.
- Hold on the completed state so viewers can read it.

CTA standard:
- Direct creator-ad native CTAs are allowed: `If you're a 5k+ TikTok Shop creator, click the button.`, `Click the button on the left corner to make your own money.`
- Preferred strong CTA: `If you're a 5K+ TikTok Shop creator, click the button. Make your own money.`
- Do not imply guaranteed results.

Money proof:
- Use money or time numbers only when the input provides them as approved creator proof, testimonial, screenshot, or campaign material.
- Examples such as `$8,000 first month`, `$13,000 a month`, `$80,000 a month`, `$130,000 in one month`, `$10K a month`, and `4 hrs -> 20 mins` are allowed only as observed proof.
- Do not invent new numbers. Do not write `you will make X`.
